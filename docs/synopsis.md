# Synopsis

Built while translating, in script order (script/scenario/NN.json = UNN g23 entry NN). Spoilers. Add each block's
summary after translating it.

## Background
- The Great Archipelago Earthquake hit Japan; the Egubado (荏狗覇菟), an army made up entirely of Guardian users,
  seized Tokyo and sealed it in a barrier. Ordinary people cannot see Guardians, so police and the Self-Defense Forces
  are powerless.
- Seven dragons (Sky, Earth, Flame, Sea, Holy, Wind, Thunder) protect Japan through dragon veins; the Egubado have
  seized the veins. Without them, Japan is fated to fall.
- Egubado ranks: Ryukihi (prophetess) > the Four Gate Generals Suzaku (Tomomi Nanjo), Byakko (Reiko Saionji), Seiryu
  (Satomi Azuma), Genbu (Megumi Hokuto) > officers such as Yumi Hanzawa (Tomomi's direct subordinate) > the ten
  disciples (Furuna, Kasho, Kasen, Anaritsu, Anan, Ubari, Sharihotsu, Subodai, Mokuren, Ragora) > Tentoshu warriors.
  Captives get a control piercing that bends them to the Egubado's will.
- The Kamikado clan guarded the Aso dragon vein; nearly wiped out by the Egubado. Chikana Suo is its heir; her
  Guardian is Gao.

## 00 (Fukuoka, Tenjin High)
Tentoshu (led by Kasho) raid Tenjin High in Fukuoka and abduct students. Miyuki Amagi (bubbly, class 3-1) learns her
best friend Mayu Homura (temple family, Hakata dialect) has a Guardian, Koen, summoned with a Buddhist mantra.
Chikana Suo arrives; Miyuki summons her own Guardian on the first try and later names it Ten-chan (its tail looks like
shrimp tempura). They beat the raiders. Chikana explains Spirit and the Egubado, and leads them to Mt. Aso, where the
captives were taken. (Miyuki only cares about hot-spring eggs.) Tutorial lines: Chikana gives them Floral Tonic.

## 01 (Mt. Aso)
At the Egubado Aso base, Yumi Hanzawa hears of Kasho's defeat and an escaped prisoner whose piercing failed; Suzaku
summons Yumi to Tokyo, leaving Furuna in charge. On the way, a voice calls Mayu. Chikana explains the seven dragons;
the Flame Dragon sleeps under Aso. The girls fight Furuna's forces; the escaped girl, Suzune Ichinomiya, frees the
prisoners. They win back the Aso dragon vein. Suzune asks them to rescue her twin sister (Kotone), held in Miyazaki.
Mayu collapses and the Flame Dragon speaks to her: it dwells within her; she must reclaim every dragon vein, find
others with a dragon's soul, and awaken the Sky and Earth Dragons. They set off for Miyazaki.

## 02 (Tokyo HQ; Miyazaki, haniwa park)
Suzaku (Tomomi) chides a trembling Yumi for losing Aso ("You're my favorite, Yumi... there will be no second chance")
and allows her to kill the girls. In Miyazaki the group finds Suzune's older twin Kotone (painfully shy; Suzune is the
outgoing younger twin, laughs "nyahaha"). The twins can only summon their Guardians (Doga = Suzune, Gara = Kotone)
together. Furuna chases them again and loses again. Chikana explains feng shui: seven dragon veins, a dragon asleep in
each; the Kamikado guard them. Miyuki (and the twins) offer to help her take them all back; Chikana is touched.
Furuna's and Kasho's gender is not stated so far: keep their lines gender-neutral until the script settles it.

## 03 (ferry to Ehime / Seto Inland Sea)
On the sea, Chikana admits she thought she'd fight alone. Mayu half-confides the Flame Dragon's warning; Miyuki has
recurring dreams of Japan being crushed. They take leave from school. Yumi attacks; passenger Misato Kaibara
recognises her as her childhood friend and neighbour "Yukko", thought dead. Yumi denies knowing her. Misato summons
Mer ("Come forth from the noble azure abyss"), silent Keiko Hichiwa summons Nagi; Yumi summons Ur ("In the names of
Metran and Sabaoth") and retreats. Suzune explains control piercings.
Misato and Keiko join (Keiko: quiet, polite, oddly perceptive; Misato curt, calls her weird).

## 04 (Ehime / dragon vein; Yumi's death)
Chikana explains dragon veins: break one and the earth's balance collapses into disaster; the Great Archipelago
Earthquake followed the Egubado seizing the veins. Mayu reveals she heard the Flame Dragon; all resolve to fight.
Yumi wears no control piercing: she fights of her own will, and admits her Guardian crushed Misato's father (Dr.
Nozomu Kaibara). Yumi is defeated and dies (Ubari is her subordinate). The Sea Dragon speaks to Misato (she bears a
dragon's soul); Misato, grief-stricken, rejects it and leaves alone for revenge in Tokyo. Chikana's group heads to
Kyoto, Nijo Castle; Miyuki and Mayu go after Misato (choice). Tomomi learns Yumi is dead. Side lines: Suzune
pilfers items from Warriors ("spoils of war"); Miyuki asks Misato to be kind to Keiko.

## 05 (Tokyo HQ; Seto Ohashi bridge)
Byakko (Reiko Saionji: vain, lazy, valley-girl drawl, polishes her nails) needles Tomomi ("skinny bag of bones");
Genbu reports activity in Hokkaido and Aomori. Reiko reluctantly goes out. Miyuki and Mayu, lost, rescue Ran
Hinokage, who is searching for her friend Kaoru Kodomari, and she joins. Mayu tells Miyuki about the Flame Dragon;
they promise to always believe each other.

## 06 (Kyoto ambush)
Reiko leaves Sharihotsu (gender-neutral so far; Reiko calls them "Sharihotsu-chan") to ambush the girls and goes home.
Chikana learns of the dragon voice. The Egubado know where Misato is. Keiko senses Misato's mind and leads them.

## 07 (Misato and Keiko)
Mer (polite, formal) warns Misato she has lost the will to live. Keiko follows; she is an empath (feels people's
feelings on touch; feared since childhood; family lost in the Great Archipelago Earthquake, alone with Nagi; born
1999, same age as Misato). She saw her old self in Misato's hatred; Misato takes her hand. Miyuki and Mayu arrive;
Misato rejoins and calls Keiko her friend. Ran (looking for Kaoru) tells Misato she'll keep searching, even in her
heart. Reiko is summoned by Suzaku after another defeat.

## 08 (third dragon vein)
Ubari seeks revenge for Yumi; Misato: "You don't know anything." Keiko hears the Wind Dragon (she bears a dragon's
soul). Ran asks Chikana to teach her to fight. Suzune keeps pilfering items.

## 09-10 (Hokkaido: Yuko, Yukari, Yui; Mt. Taisetsu)
New party. Yuko Daichi (brash, short-tempered, hates hassle; on a solo trip; standard speech, not Hakata) is
recruited by Yukari Hijiri (ex-Self-Defense Forces, cool, pushy, teasing) and names her rock Guardian Pochi (Yukari
suggested "Boulder"). Yukari explains Guardians (= defensive instinct given form), Spirit, orders. Kasen attacks.
Hasumi Naoe (Yukari's friend, a woman; firm, masculine speech) escorts Yui Iwamizawa (gentle; her family guarded the
Taisetsu dragon vein, her presence sealed it in blizzards) and is captured by Seiryu (Satomi Azuma); taken to Mt.
Osore. The Holy Dragon speaks to Yukari. Yui summons Saiki (sword-shaped, selfish, moody Guardian: "Five-ringed sword
wherein life dwells...").

## 11 (Hokkaido, toward Mt. Osore)
Yui explains the seven dragon veins as Japan's blood vessels. Yukari: the Great Archipelago Earthquake was caused by
the Egubado destroying dragon veins; her SDF superiors wouldn't believe in Guardians, so she quit to investigate.
Anaritsu (female, あたし, short-tempered) and Subodai ambush them; their drafted Guardian user, Sayaka Shizunai
(20, college girl, airhead drawl "li-ike", "nothing to do with i-it"; piercing didn't take), joins. Sayaka calls
Yukari an "old lady"; Yukari is 18.

## 12 (Mt. Osore: fake Naoe, Kaoru, the split)
Ryoko Kagami (Genbu's officer; short, boastful, cowardly brat, ボク; "Call me Kagami-sama!"; hates being called
"shorty") disguises herself as Hasumi; Yukari unmasks her ("she'd never call herself 'boku'"). Kaoru Kodomari
(tomboy girl, mistaken for a boy: "little guy"; searching for her kidnapped friend Ran-chan) summons Fudo (lazy,
crude: "Fudo Myoo, Ashuranata!" mantra) and joins. Saiki (Yui calls her "Hime-chan"; speaks of herself as "Saiki")
can track Kagami's aura. Yukari reveals she heard the Holy Dragon and wants to free the dragon veins instead of
chasing Kagami. Choice "I'll help you" / "I don't buy it" splits the party (Yuko follows Yukari or goes with Yui).

## 13-14 (Kagami's traps; Ruri, Akira)
Kagami's bomb trap (three bombs, robot countdown); Ruri Hiraizumi (boy-crazy, just dumped, "third one this year";
Guardian Kirara; calls Yui "big sis") joins. Anan (Kagami's long-suffering subordinate) gets bullied by Ruri.
Yuko theorises a Guardian reflects its master's wishes (Saiki's selfishness = Yui's wish to be free; Yui is the
last of the Iwamizawa line). Operation Dam-Dam: Kagami's detonator has no batteries. Kagami reveals Jin (Hitomi Jin)
took Naoe to Sado. Akira Ikazuchi ("wandering hero", earnest stiff hero speech: "Yes indeed!", "on behalf of the
heavens!") helps and leaves.

## 15-16 (stone circle teleporter; base self-destruct)
Hitomi Jin (Genbu's officer, cool, masculine; Genbu's favourite; Ragora her subordinate) covers for Kagami, holds the real
Hasumi on Sado and leaves for Nikko (blowing up the teleport room). Yukari's group finds a stone circle teleporter; Akira helps again (Sayaka: "a heroine, not a
hero"). Yuko tells Yukari to lean on them. The teleporter lands them in an Egubado base that self-destructs (robot
countdown, "IMMEDIATE DETONATION MODE"); Ruri (with Kirara, a sharp-tongued female Guardian) is freed and joins.
Genbu (Megumi Hokuto: stern, masculine) scolds Seiryu (Satomi Azuma: catty) for handing the prisoner to Kagami.

## 17-18 (Sado reunion; Kagami quits; toward Nikko)
They were sent to Sado. Both halves of the party reunite and make up (Yuko: "Yukari, you idiot! ...and sorry I'm so
short-tempered"); Hasumi rescued. Akira (a rock musician, "rocker"; Guardian Zabi-tan, crackles with electricity)
joins. Yukari tells Hasumi she heard the Holy Dragon: her mission is to free the veins and awaken the Sky and Earth
Dragons. Genbu orders Jin to settle it at Nikko. Kagami's last-chance attack fails; she quits the Egubado ("back to
being a bum"). Next: Nikko dragon vein, then Tokyo (Egubado HQ).

## 19 (Nikko Toshogu: all parties meet)
Miyuki's (Kyushu) and Yukari's (Hokkaido) groups meet at Toshogu. Jin fights them, loses, and keeps her word: she
will guide them to Tokyo; Miyuki trusts her, Misato refuses ("she's Egubado!"). Akira dreams of the Thunder Dragon
(the fifth Dragon Wielder; she thinks it means a hero "transformation"). Five Dragon Wielders gathered: Mayu (Flame),
Yukari (Holy), Misato (Sea), Keiko (Wind), Akira (Thunder); mission: free the veins, awaken the Sky and Earth Dragons.
Misato: only five veins outside Tokyo, so where is the seventh? Miyuki and Yuko feel they've met before. Kaoru and
Ran reunite. Yui (only child) envies the twins.

## 20-21 (Tokyo Tower; toward the National Diet Building)
Jin's route: a warp from Toshogu straight to Tokyo Tower. Two veins lie at the Diet Building ("where two energies
collide"). Jin explains Ryukihi's creed (Japan will sink; her worshippers' souls go to bliss and are reborn).
Chikana, Hasumi, Yui and Jin scout; the rest wait at Hibiya Park. Misato grudgingly "hires" Jin.

## 22-23 (Diet scouting; Hibiya Park detour)
The Four Gate Generals aren't at the Diet Building: suspicious. Chikana fears she isn't a Dragon Wielder and will
be dead weight against Ryukihi (only the Seven Dragons can beat her). Yuko leads the others on a park tour of Tokyo
(Shakujii Park...). An Egubado squad's hired "Master", Takegami (old warrior, ワシ/お主, lives to fight the strong),
loses and joins to fight Ryukihi. Suzune finds a toy hero belt; Akira begs for it.

## 24-25 (Hibiya Park camp; the Four Gates)
Yukari tells the legend of the Emperor Dragon (皇龍: the Seven Dragons released from the veins become one and save
Japan); she thinks it means the Dragon Wielders themselves. Ruri falls for Takegami and asks him to be her "partner";
he hears "sparring partner". Misato and Yuko quarrel while worrying about the scouts; everyone goes to look. Camp
vignettes: Keiko's habitual polite speech, Miyuki and Keiko "super good friends"; Yukari gives Mayu spare shoes and
worries she seems bossy (Yuko: no regrets about following her); Suzune asks Keiko to mind-read the newcomers.
Sayaka goes to Aomori Gakuin Women's College. At the Diet Building the scouts are spotted; the others arrive in time.
A warrior: no one nears the Diet Building while the Four Gates are guarded.

## 25-26 (the Four Gates plan)
Jin: Tokyo's Four Gates (earth-vein energy generators: north Shinjuku Central Park, west Setagaya Tonder Egg, east
Asakusa Sensoji, south Tokyo Second International Airport) now power a barrier around the Diet Building; they
restore each other, so all four stone towers must be destroyed at the same moment: the party splits into four
groups. The Four Gate Generals wait at the gates (Genbu still expects Jin to see through it; Reiko blames Genbu for
Jin's betrayal). Akira drags shy Kotone into her hero roll call ("That was embarrassing...").
Group-split vignettes: Sayaka wants a gate with fun on the side; Misato refuses to team with her; Ran gives Keiko an
item "so you'll be teamed with Misato".

## 27-30 (the four gate battles)
Each gate has a shared pre-battle roll call (every party member's one-liner, repeated per gate: keep them identical)
and a "stone tower" line each. 27 (Shinjuku): Genbu (Megumi) vs Jin: they joined hands to protect Japan from "the
rotten people of this world"; Megumi can't forget her miserable childhood; Jin: "You're only taking revenge on the
world." Suzune proposes practising separate summons (she couldn't save Kotone when kidnapped; Kotone is 19). Yui
tells Ruri her "lovey-dovey attack" on Takegami would fail. 28 (Setagaya Tonder Egg): Reiko (Miyuki calls her "old
lady") preaches death as bliss; Mayu: "Kyushu dialect's the whole world's common tongue!" Sayaka pities Miyuki's
boyfriendless life. Yukari plays "healer" so Chikana charges. 29 (airport): Seiryu (Satomi) vs Akira, who mistakes
her grand idioms for a rival's style ("I'd expect no less from the Four Gate Generals"); Sayaka confuses punk and
thrash metal; Takegami leaves Seiryu to Yuko; Hasumi wants a rematch from Taisetsu. 30 (Sensoji): Tomomi (Suzaku)
"raised" Yumi ("my darling little bird"); Misato's rage. Mayu encourages Ran: Miyuki has a sharp sense for these things.

## 31-34 (the Seven Dragons awaken; Hiryuki Palace)
Yuko and Miyuki felt a bond on meeting. The last dragon vein is freed: every Dragon Wielder's Guardian becomes its
dragon (Ten-chan = Sky Dragon, speaks: "Please call me Sky Dragon, Miyuki"; Pochi = Earth Dragon; so Miyuki is the
Sky Dragon, Yuko the Earth Dragon). Ryukihi is at Hiryuki Palace, an island fortress in Tokyo Bay. Vignettes: Yui on
Guardians (Ten-chan needn't talk); Jin and Keiko (purity hurts the tainted); Takegami's musings fool Ruri into
expecting a confession. Hasumi hoped to be a Dragon Wielder (foreshadowing). A Black Dragon appears to Jin. 33: the
Four Gate Generals return; the fortress will fly and drive itself as a wedge into Japan's greatest dragon vein under
Tokyo Bay, sinking Japan. Jin knew and kept quiet: "I bet everything on you." 34: the Generals chant a mantra
("On a un ra ken sowaka!").

## 35-36 (inside the flying fortress; the rearguard holds the giant Guardian)
35: Miyuki, Mayu, Yuko, Misato, Akira, Yukari and Keiko are teleported onto Hiryuki Palace, now airborne and heading
for Japan's greatest dragon vein. Chikana, Jin, Suzune, Kotone and the rest stay behind as the rearguard. Miyuki cries
about "everybody not here" and calls the others cold; Yuko snaps ("Of course we are!"); Mayu scolds her with tough love
("Where'd the girl who cheered everybody up go?"): the others stayed because they believe in them. Keiko realises Jin
knew all along and meant to be the decoy. Akira: "I can't face them unless I win and go back." Yukari: "Think only
about fighting." Tone: shaky, then resolve; Mayu blunt and affectionate.
36: The rearguard (Chikana, Jin, Hasumi, Yui, Suzune, Kotone, Ruri, Sayaka, Kaoru, Ran, Takegami) fights the fortress's
giant Guardian; the Four Gate Generals gloat ("how many will be left? Two? Three?" - Megumi). Kaoru: "Like Yuko and the
others would ever lose!" Banter: Sayaka "I don't wanna die ye-et", plans to see Akira's live show; Chikana and Hasumi
joke about crybaby Miyuki; Suzune/Yui bicker. 36.017-030 (repeated as 38.048-058) are defeat lines (Chikana "I'm not
up to it, huh", Ran/Kaoru farewell, Jin "Seven Dragons... I leave it to you."): keep the two copies identical.

## 37 (the endless guards)
The upper party fights wave after wave of Royal Guards. Mayu hesitates ("Maybe I should just... No. It's nothin'."):
a sacrifice impulse. Misato to Yuko: "...I don't dislike you, you know." Yukari frets they'll reach the dragon vein
before Ryukihi, slips "If only Naoe were here...", then: "I won't say it again" (her one crack).

## 38 (the dummy Master; the Generals squabble)
The rearguard is worn out. Jin reasons: one Guardian, one Master; the Generals' combined will forms a dummy Master,
the "core", in the middle of the body. Ranged attackers aim there; Takegami: "Hmm, interesting!" Tomomi: if little
girls can beat Ryukihi-sama she is no more than that ("noble, beautiful, and above all, absolutely strong": faith in
strength, not love); Megumi furious and worried for Jin; Satomi tells her to stop fretting over Jin.

## 39 (more guards; Yukari's shaking)
Mayu and Akira fall behind. Yukari barks ("We can't be soft"); Misato sees through her: "has your body stopped shaking
yet?" "Not yet." "You always get the thankless role. Want to cry?" "I don't have time for that." Keiko wishes she
could see the future. "Just how many floors does this place have?"

## 40 (Sky and Earth lead)
Yuko and Miyuki end up alone. Yukari's words come back: the Sky and Earth Dragons lead, "we need you two to try
especially hard". Carefree Yuko, for once: "Right now, I'm really, really angry... We're gonna beat Ryukihi!"

## 41 (Ryukihi's Ryuki; the Seven Dragons assemble)
Ryukihi and her Guardian Ryuki ("grant them the purification called death"); the two alone can't hurt it. Ryukihi
calls the Sky and Earth Dragons back to her ("Accept me!"); they hold on until the others arrive and each calls her
dragon in turn (Sky, Earth, Flame, Sea, Thunder, Wind, Holy: glossary names, short shouts). Ryukihi: "The Seven
Dragons, which were once one...!"

## 42 (Ryukihi fuses; Hiryuki Palace falls)
Ryukihi fuses with Ryuki (True Ryuki). "Do you wish to watch your own country rot away? Japan has no future." Miyuki:
"It does! It does have a future!" Yuko: "From now on... we'll change it." Ryukihi, dying: "Someday, you too... will
understand..." The palace begins to collapse. Tone: Ryukihi regal and archaic, not snarling; the girls' answer is the
theme (future vs rot). The scenario ends here: the escape, the Jin / Black Dragon payoff and the ending are FMV
(STAFF.STR and the epilogue movies, D3), not g23 text; 43-49 are drafts/copies, 50-65 tutorials.
